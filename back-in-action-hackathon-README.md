# Back in Action - Eleniak Hackathon Case Study

## Overview

**Back in Action** was a team concept developed during the Eleniak Hackathon in Birmingham, held from 15 to 17 July 2026.

The 2.5-day event brought participants together to research, design and present a product concept. Our team explored a privacy-focused telehealth service intended to make erectile-dysfunction care more accessible, convenient and clinically responsible.

> This was a collaborative hackathon project. The research, product thinking, commercial analysis and presentation were developed collectively by the team. This repository does not claim sole authorship by any individual participant.

## Event details

- **Event:** Eleniak Hackathon
- **Dates:** 15-17 July 2026
- **Duration:** 2.5 days
- **Venue:** The Jointworks, Jewellery Quarter, Birmingham, United Kingdom
- **Project:** Back in Action
- **Format:** Team research, product development and final presentation
- **Outcome:** The concept was presented but was not selected as a winning project

## The problem

People experiencing erectile dysfunction may delay seeking medical help because of embarrassment, privacy concerns, uncertainty about treatment and the inconvenience of arranging an in-person appointment.

The team examined how a digital service could reduce these barriers while maintaining clinical oversight and responsible communication.

## Proposed solution

Back in Action was designed as a direct-to-customer telehealth concept offering:

- A secure online assessment
- Review by licensed clinicians
- A personalised treatment pathway
- Discreet medication delivery, where clinically appropriate
- Ongoing clinical support
- Privacy-focused and educational communication

The concept was created for a proposed launch in Florida, considering its telehealth readiness and target customer profile.

## Work completed during the hackathon

The team collaborated across:

- Problem definition and customer research
- Customer-persona development
- Competitor and alternative-solution analysis
- Service and customer-journey design
- Business-model development
- Unit-economics considerations
- Advertising concepts and responsible messaging
- Final presentation development and delivery

The advertising concepts focused on three themes: confidence, clinical trust, and privacy and convenience.

## Key learning

The hackathon provided practical experience in moving from an open-ended healthcare problem to a structured product concept within a limited timeframe. Important lessons included:

1. Sensitive healthcare products require trust, privacy and clinical responsibility to be treated as core product requirements.
2. A strong concept must connect user needs, service design, commercial viability and responsible communication.
3. Time-limited collaboration requires clear prioritisation and rapid alignment across the team.
4. Presenting an early-stage concept requires transparency about assumptions, limitations and work still needed.
